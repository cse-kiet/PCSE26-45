**LocaTrack: Prophet-Based Time-Series Forecasting
for Crime Trend Prediction**

Due to fast urban growth and movement of people
toward cities, along with changes in crime trends, crime forecasting has recently become a significant application for lawenforcement agencies. The aim of this study is to find out whether
we can discover with an interpretable model like Prophet, an
easily understandable pattern about what happens in terms of
seasonality, anomaly periods, and crime trends without having
to use complex black-box algorithms. Prophet is employed to
estimate the long-term trend and weekly seasonality after a series
of methodical preprocessing of the dataset, handling missing
data, normalization of time-related features, and decomposition
into different time series components. The model produced interpretable, distinguishable factors that each related meaningfully to
extant evidence-based offending behavior and were robust across
multiple evaluation periods. The findings indicate that Prophet
provides a good trade-off of interpretability and predictive
performance, making it suitable for practical operational environments where transparent prediction is required. In future work,
we will further develop this approach with demographic and
environment features, model spatial hotspots, and develop hybrid
models involving Prophet with machine learning technologies for
improved precision.
Index Terms—crime forecasting, Prophet time-series model,
crime trends, seasonality patterns, anomaly detection, data preprocessing, interpretable predictions, law-enforcement analysis.
